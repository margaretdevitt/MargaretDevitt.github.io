Originally hailing from Raleigh, North Carolina, Margaret Devitt is an aspiring librarian with interests in the academic sector. She received her Bachelor’s in English in 2024 from Meredith College, with minors in Professional Writing and Business Administration. Currently she is in the process of obtaining her Master’s in Library and Information Science and a certificate in Instructional Design from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro. Additionally, she works as a graduate assistant at the Jackson Library reference desk, where she assists patrons, answers chats and calls, and gains on-the-job experience in her field.

Included on this website are a copy of Margaret’s resume, her contact information for professional inquiries, and a perusable gallery of some of her most recent projects focused on information organization and instructional design.
